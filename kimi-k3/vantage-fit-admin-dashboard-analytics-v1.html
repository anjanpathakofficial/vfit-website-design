Rollout questions HR asks first

Can I see a specific employee's health data?

No. Individual weight, BMI, and health assessment results are never exposed to HR. Every surface in the admin dashboard shows counts, rates, and trends across groups, never a person's health profile.

Which parts are tied to an annual plan?

Wellness Leagues and the League Report are annual-plan surfaces; other tiers see them grayscale-locked with Contact Account Manager. KPI cards, filters, Recommended Actions, certificates, and the Employee, Transaction, and Redemption reports are standard.

Do the AI insights change anything in my program?

No. Leadership Insights are read-only observations from aggregated data. Acting on them is your call, usually through a Recommended Action link.
