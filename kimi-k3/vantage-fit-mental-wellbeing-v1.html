What this is not.

Not therapy

This is a guided audio content library. It makes no clinical or therapeutic claim and does not replace care.

Not unlimited

A fixed, curated catalog of 30+ sessions. We do not pretend to be a consumer streaming library.

Not AI-curated

No recommendation engine and no personalization claims. Employees browse categories and pick.

Phone and app only

No wearable required. Playback and logging live in the mobile app; admins see aggregate counts, never who played what.

Before rollout

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sessions download as MP3 and play without a connection. Completions batch-sync to the activity log when the phone is back online.

Can HR see who listened to what?

No. Admins see aggregate mindfulness minutes and participation counts. An individual’s sessions stay private to that employee.

Can we add our own sessions?

Select organizations can request additional or tailored sessions through their account manager. The core seven-category library ships as standard.
