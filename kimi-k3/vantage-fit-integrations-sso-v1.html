Module 1 · For HR

The watch they already own is the tracker.

No one needs to buy hardware: steps come from Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android, and a wearable is a self-serve option, not a requirement.

Phone, no wearable

Apple Health and Google Fit

Steps, workouts, weight, calories, sleep, heart rate, and distance on iOS. Steps from Google Fit by default on Android. An Apple Watch syncs automatically through the paired iPhone.

Syncs on app open

Fitbit

Steps, workouts, heart-rate zones, and sleep stages over OAuth. Syncs on app open and on demand, with up to 30 days of history. Not a real-time feed.

Real-time webhook

Garmin

The only real-time integration: webhook push, not periodic polling, with daily and activity summaries plus GPS routes. Tokens expire after 60 days, so employees reconnect when prompted.

Via Apple Health or Android

Samsung Watch and 70+ apps

Samsung Watch connects as an Android device option. Through Apple Health, 70+ trusted third-party apps count too: Amazfit and Zepp, Mi Band, Huawei, Nike Run Club, Strava.

One primary device, one clean number.

Each employee picks one primary device. A connected Fitbit or Garmin overrides the phone as the step source, and trusted step-source filtering keeps stray sources out of challenge counts. A Garmin run or a phone-only step day lands on the same participation score.

If your company already runs Vantage Circle rewards and recognition, employees reach Vantage Fit with the same account. No second login.

Wearables FAQ

Do employees need a fitness tracker to take part?

No. Steps come from Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android. Connecting a Fitbit, Garmin, Apple Watch, or Samsung Watch is optional and self-serve.

SSO and HRIS FAQ

Can we turn on SSO ourselves?

No. SSO is SAML 2.0, set up through an IdP metadata exchange with the integration team, with lead time dependent on your IdP.

Devices and identity are plumbing: they put every employee on one surface, so every synced log becomes the same participation number HR reports upstairs.
