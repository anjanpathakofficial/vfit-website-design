Company-scoped multi-tenant isolation

Every query carries your company ID. Lab and health-risk queries are company-scoped by design, so one tenant's data never surfaces inside another's reports.

One account across the suite

Companies running other Vantage Circle products share the same user, company, and auth layer, so there is one identity boundary to review, not several.

HR sees the crowd, never the person.

The participation number is usable upstairs because individual health never leaves the employee. That boundary is a product rule, not a policy promise.

Offboarding that forgets the person, not the record.

When an employee leaves or asks to be deleted, the person goes and the reporting stays intact.

Account deletion

Hashed, cleared, kept countable

Name and email are hashed, tokens are cleared, and historical activity and transaction records are retained for reporting. Deletion behavior is company-configurable.

HIPAA scope

A BAA for the lab pipeline only

The platform is HIPAA-guideline aligned. For companies using Health Data Upload, BAA provisions for HIPAA compliance apply to that lab-report pipeline, not to the whole product. See health data upload

Can HR see an individual employee's health data?

No. HR sees aggregate org and department metrics only. Individual health profiles, HRA answers, and lab values are never shown, and health-risk targeting returns a count, not a list of names.

Where is our data hosted?

In one of four regional instances: India, US, EU, or UAE. The region is chosen at onboarding with in-region endpoints, and changing it later requires a migration.

Is Vantage Fit HIPAA compliant?

The platform is HIPAA-guideline aligned. BAA provisions for HIPAA compliance apply specifically to the lab-report pipeline for companies that use Health Data Upload, not to the whole product.
