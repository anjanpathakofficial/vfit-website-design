Questions buyers ask

Before you book.

What counts as participation?

For the headline metric, participation should mean that an enrolled employee completed at least one qualifying wellness action during the rolling 30-day window. The exact qualifying actions should be documented and consistent.

Is Vantage Fit only for step challenges?

No. Programs can include multiple activity types, active minutes, team formats, mindfulness, content and other wellness actions, depending on the selected plan and configuration.

Where does the Organizational Wellness Score fit?

It is a supporting composite that helps HR explore participation, activity improvement, program adherence and available health signals. Participation remains the clearer headline for program adoption.

Can we start with one challenge?

Yes. Vantage Fit offers single-challenge and unlimited year-round options, with plan and volume pricing shown on the current pricing page.
