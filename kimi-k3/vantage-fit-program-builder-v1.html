Before you build

Do employees need a wearable?

No. Steps come from the phone or a connected device. No format requires a wearable.

Is drag-and-drop available on every format?

No. Drag-and-drop task configuration is Custom Challenge only.

Can I offer cash prizes?

Point and monetary rewards are account-manager gated and live for one company today. Certificates are self-serve on every challenge.
