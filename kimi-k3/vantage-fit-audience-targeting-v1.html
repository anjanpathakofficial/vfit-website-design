Health-risk targeting is a count, not a list.

When you create a challenge, a health-risk audience answers with one number. Any targeted challenge is automatically marked private, so it never appears in a public browse list.

Risk codes are categories, not biomarker thresholds:

DiabetesHeartLiverKidney ThyroidBlood healthVitamin D / B12Inflammation

No screen and no export exposes who matched. The admin plans a heart-health challenge for about a thousand people and never learns which thousand.

Enrollment is admin work, not an invitation.

Employees do not browse and self-join. People get into a targeted challenge two ways, and the leaderboard treats late arrivals fairly.

1Admin adds directly Search and add individuals, or upload a bulk CSV. Done in one sitting.
2Audience rules enroll Set the rule once. Everyone who matches is enrolled instantly, including new hires who match later.
3Late joiners start clean Leaderboard score counts from the join or add date forward. No retroactive credit, no penalty.

Before you aim

Can I target by activity level?

No. Targeting covers country, city, department, gender, age range, language, and, with Workforce Health enabled, health-risk codes. Activity level is not a targeting dimension.

Can I see who is in a health-risk cohort?

No. The match returns a count only, with the privacy disclaimer on screen. No screen or export exposes identities.

Can employees join a targeted challenge themselves?

No. Targeted challenges are automatically private. Employees get in when an admin adds them or an audience rule matches them.
