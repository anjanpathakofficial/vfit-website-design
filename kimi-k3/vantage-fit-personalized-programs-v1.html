Two systems. One Programs tab.

Training Plans and the Content Library do different jobs, so we keep them separate. One is a structured path the employee owns. The other is a catalog HR can publish from and challenges can draw on.

System one Training Plans

A VFit-managed catalog of structured, week-by-week plans. The employee enrolls, tunes the schedule, and runs it as a personal challenge. HR does not create, run, or stop these.

System two Content Library

Articles, videos, podcasts, webinars, Health Bites, and quizzes that HR publishes and targets. Any item can become a challenge task with automatic completion tracking.

Three questions, then next Monday.

Enrollment tunes the plan to the employee’s week, shows a week-by-week preview, and starts the following Monday. The plan then lives in the Challenges tab as a personal challenge, tracked by auto-sync or manual entry like any other.

  1. 1
    How many weeks?A slider from 4 to 8 weeks sets the plan length.
  2. 2
    How many days a week?Three to six training days, picked as a radio choice.
  3. 3
    Which days?Checkboxes place the sessions; unselected days stay rest days.

A training plan runs in the Challenges tab as a personal challenge. No leaderboard, no certificate, and only the employee can quit it. HR sees a read-only ongoing-programs report, nothing more.

Content Library: the follow-through.

HR publishes and targets the catalog itself once the Programs tab is on. Formats cover quick reads to watch-along sessions, and every item can carry a quiz or become a challenge task.

Content as a task

Watching, reading, or finishing a Health Bite logs a content activity, so a three-minute lesson counts toward participation like any other task.

Quizzes that grade themselves

Radio and checkbox questions, all-correct-to-pass. Passing or failing both record the attempt; the log keeps the honest version.

Targeted by health profile

Content reaches audiences by demographics like age, gender, and health profile. Never by how active someone is.

A Marketplace section also surfaces partner offerings through external links with a disclaimer.

Who controls what.

Training Plans: select-partner

Enabled per company through your account manager. Plans are VFit-managed; admins cannot create, modify, or stop them.

Content: self-serve

Once the Programs tab is on, admins create, publish, and unpublish articles, videos, and podcasts, and build quiz questions and categories.

Ops-required extras

Marketplace, Health Bites creation, multi-language versions, and demographic targeting are set up with the Vantage Fit team.

Lite Mode

Lite clients get no Programs tab by default. Programs surfaces only where a section is enabled.

Before rollout

Can HR create or customize a training plan?

No. The catalog is managed by Vantage Fit: two plans available, seven coming soon. Admins cannot author plans, edit them, or switch individual plans off per company today.

Can HR stop an employee’s plan?

No. A training plan is a personal challenge. Only the employee can quit it, and HR’s visibility is a read-only report of ongoing programs.

Do employees get a certificate for finishing a plan?

No. Training plans are personal and non-competitive, so there is no completion certificate. HR-run challenges are the ones that award certificates.
