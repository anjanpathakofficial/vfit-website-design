Turn a lab report into a private, personal plan.

Employees upload their annual health-check report. AI extraction bands about 104 biomarkers by risk and turns it into guidance only they can see. Workforce health risk analytics for HR stops at org-level prevalence.

Availability first

Annual partner module, enabled per company by your account manager. Currently limited to selected organisations running health check-up programs.

About 104 biomarkers, banded by risk.

Coverage spans about 14 health areas, from metabolic and heart to thyroid, liver, kidney, blood health, inflammation, and Vitamin D and B12.

HR gets prevalence, never a person.

Workforce health risk analytics answer one question: what share of the workforce was flagged at risk in each health area. That is the whole view.

Intake is employee self-upload once enabled. A bulk SFTP intake exists as a one-company arrangement, not a standard offering.

Not department slices

Prevalence is reported for the whole organization. It is not broken down by department, team, or location.

Not a diagnosis

Risk bands describe where a result sits against its reference range. They are not medical advice.

Not self-serve

Enabled per company by the account manager for annual partners on health check-up programs.

Who gets Health Data Upload?

Annual partners running health check-up programs. It is a premium whitelist module, enabled per company by the account manager, and currently limited to selected organisations.

Can HR see my lab report?

No. HR sees org-level prevalence only: the percent of the workforce flagged at risk per health area. No screen or export exposes an individual report.

Is the AI summary medical advice?

No. The extraction summarizes results and bands them by risk. Suggestions are general wellness guidance, not medical advice.
