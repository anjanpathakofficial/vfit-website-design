Designed for follow-through

Built so week three feels like week one.

Four mechanics, working together, that keep a long program alive after the launch-day buzz wears off.

01 01

Balanced by design

You set the points per task, and the scoring keeps any single task, or athlete, from running away with it.

  • Non-step tasks cap at 100% of target, so over-logging earns nothing extra
  • Steps cap at 25,000 a day by default, adjustable for your program
  • Team scores average member effort, so every person's contribution counts
02 02

Habits or totals, your call

Each task runs in the mode that fits the behavior you want to build.

  • Daily mode: "hit the target on at least 4 days this week" builds consistency
  • Weekly mode: "accumulate 35,000 steps" rewards sustained effort
  • Different tasks, different weeks, so the program never repeats itself
03 03

Recognition beyond the podium

Only three people win the leaderboard. Everyone else still needs a reason to finish.

  • Automatic badges mark personal milestones from the first week onward
  • Branded completion certificates carry your logo, seal, and signature
  • Score breakdowns per task and week keep progress visible
04 04

Rewards worth earning

Effort converts to points, and points convert to things people actually want.

  • Points credit the moment a task completes, with a confirmation in-app
  • Redeemable for gift cards from country-relevant catalogs
  • Set values in USD; employees see them in local currency

Competition optional. Employees can opt out of the leaderboard and still earn every point, badge, and certificate.

A multi-activity program touches sleep, mood, weight, and lab results. So the line between what HR sees and what stays private is drawn by the product, not by policy promises.

What HR sees

FAQ

Questions before rollout.

Do employees have to log everything by hand?

No. Steps, distance, calories, and active minutes sync automatically from phones and wearables. Daily habits like water or meditation are one tap, and content tasks complete themselves when watched or read. Manual step entry exists only as a fallback, if your company enables it.

Can different activities really share one leaderboard?

Yes. Each task is worth points you set, non-step tasks cap at 100% of target, and steps cap daily. Team scores average member effort, so a few power walkers cannot carry a squad, and the yogi can outscore the runner.

Can we brand the program and add our own activities?

Yes. Name and theme every week with your own logos and colors, create custom activities like "Office stretch break," add daily adherence check-ins like "Took vitamins," and issue completion certificates with your logo and signature.

What about privacy when sleep and mood are involved?

Admins see participation and aggregate trends only. Individual mood logs, sleep patterns, weight, HRA answers, and lab reports are never visible to HR, and employees can opt out of leaderboards entirely while still earning points.

How long does it take to launch?

Template programs carry themes, tasks, targets, and scoring, so most teams launch in days. Set dates, pick your audience, publish. Your account manager can also help design your first multi-week program.
