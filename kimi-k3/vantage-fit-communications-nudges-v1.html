The quiet 40% gets a nudge, not a campaign.

Every program has a silent middle. Call it the quiet 40%. That figure is a framing device, not a Vantage Fit statistic, and the fix is a rule, not a blast.

Before you hit send

Can I switch one email type off myself?

Not yet. The master email switch and per-type toggles are account-manager mediated today. Self-serve toggles are on the backlog.

Are the nudges written by AI?

No. Recommended Actions are rule-based, and email and push copy is templated, not AI-personalized.

Do employees on the web app get push?

No. Push notifications with deep links go to the iOS and Android apps. On web, employees get emails.
