How it works

Every step becomes distance.

Employees walk, run, or wheel as they normally do. Vantage Fit converts steps to kilometers on a live leaderboard.

01 01

Move as you normally do

Steps sync from the phone or wearable employees already have. Distance comes from step count, not GPS, so no routes to plan and no workouts to start.

02 02

Watch the distance add up

1,000 steps becomes a kilometer by default, on a ratio and in units (km or miles) you choose. A lunch walk counts as much as a morning run.

FAQ

Questions HR teams ask.

Do employees have to run, or use GPS?

No. Everyday steps convert to distance automatically, so walking the dog, commuting, and lunch breaks all count. There are no routes to follow, no GPS workouts to start, and no running required. Distance is calculated from step count, not location tracking.

Do employees need a fitness tracker?

No. iPhones and Android phones count steps on their own through Apple Health and Google Fit, so anyone with a smartphone can join. Employees who have them can connect Fitbit or Garmin directly, and Apple Watch plus most popular bands sync through Apple Health. One primary device is active at a time, so steps never double-count.

One day or multi-week: which should we pick?

Pick a one-day marathon for a shared moment tied to a date, like a program launch or World Heart Day. Pick a multi-week E-Marathon (2 to 4 weeks works well) for distance campaigns and themed pushes. Many teams pair them: a one-day kickoff, then a multi-week main event.

Can our global offices join the same event?

Yes, that's the default. Start and end times localize to each participant's midnight, so a one-day marathon is one true calendar day in every time zone. Rewards configured in USD display in each employee's local currency with a country-relevant gift-card catalogue across 190+ countries.

How do the distance tiers work?

Employees choose the target that motivates them: 5K, 10K, half marathon, full marathon, or a custom distance you set. Rewards scale with the target automatically, so bigger efforts earn more and every fitness level has a real finish line.

What can we report afterward?

During the event, the admin dashboard tracks check-ins, distance covered, and finishers live. Afterward, export the leaderboard by tier, department, or country, a participation report with one row per enrolled employee, and a full points transaction ledger, all as CSVs.
