Quick answers

What buyers ask on this page

Do employees need the mobile app, or does the web version work?

iOS and Android carry the full experience. Web covers steps, limited manual logging, and the admin dashboard, plus lab-report upload for clients with Health Data Upload enabled.

What is Lite Mode?

A steps-only mode. The logging surface collapses to step counts and race-style challenges, and every card flagged Not in Lite Mode stays hidden until you move to Full Mode.

Who can use Health Data Upload, and who sees the reports?

It is an annual, whitelist-enabled pipeline. AI extracts lab values from uploaded PDFs, and HR sees cohort prevalence, never an individual report.
