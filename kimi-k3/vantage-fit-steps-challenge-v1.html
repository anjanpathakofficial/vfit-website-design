The familiar story

Most step challenges peak in week one.

A launch-day spike, a quiet leaderboard by week two, and a participation number you'd rather not present. It isn't motivation that's missing — it's mechanics.

Manual tracking kills momentum

If joining means installing a pedometer app and typing in totals, most of the company opts out by day three.

The same ten people win

When only raw step totals count, your marathoners dominate and everyone else stops checking the leaderboard.

Nothing to come back for

One bad day shouldn't end someone's challenge. Without streaks, milestones, and rewards, it does.

No proof it worked

A screenshot of a spreadsheet isn't a result. Leadership asks who participated and what changed — and the answer is usually a shrug.

Every one of these is a design problem. Vantage Fit's step challenges are built around the fixes: automatic tracking, fair scoring, real rewards, and reporting that holds up in a leadership review.

FAQ

Questions HR teams ask.

Do employees need a fitness tracker?

No. iPhones and Android phones count steps on their own through Apple Health and Google Fit, so anyone with a smartphone can join. Employees who have them can connect Fitbit or Garmin directly, and Apple Watch plus most popular bands sync through Apple Health. One primary device is active at a time, so steps never double-count.

Can we run one challenge across countries and time zones?

Yes — that's the default, not a special case. Start dates, end dates, and daily resets localize to each participant's midnight, so a 30-day challenge is 30 true calendar days for everyone. Rewards configured in USD display in each employee's local currency with a country-relevant gift-card catalogue.

How do teams work?

Add teams to Streak, Journey, or Custom challenges. Team scores are the average of members' effort — not the sum — so every person's steps matter and a small squad of power walkers can't dominate. Create teams in the dashboard, bulk-upload them via CSV, or let employees form their own. A separate team leaderboard runs alongside the individual one.

How long does it take to launch?

Pre-built templates carry the format, targets, scoring, and weekly themes — you set the dates, audience, and rewards. Most teams launch in days; a steps-only race with CSV enrollment can go live in 1–2 days.

What can we report when it's over?

The admin dashboard tracks participation rate, active users, and team rankings live. After the challenge, export the leaderboard (weekly or overall, by department), a participation report with one row per enrolled employee, and a full points transaction ledger for reward-budget reconciliation — all as CSVs.

What stops people from cheating?

Step data only counts from trusted sources — phone sensors and connected wearables, not manual entries. Daily and hourly step caps flag inhuman totals, and admins can inspect suspicious activity. A 3-day sync buffer after the end date lets late wearable syncs count before winners are finalized.
