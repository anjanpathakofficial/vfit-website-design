Sleep, heart rate, and weight, next to the step count.

Vantage Fit’s employee health metrics tracking platform puts vitals, sleep, and weight in the same app as steps and workouts. Every health update writes its own activity-log entry, so it counts toward participation without a second form.

Vitals, without a second form.

Blood pressure enters as “120/80” and parses into systolic and diastolic. Cholesterol and diabetes status sit alongside. Weight comes from a scroll picker or a wearable sync, stored precisely and shown in the employee’s own unit. Each update lands in the activity log on its own.

Sleep is a schedule. Heart rate has two paths.

Sleep logs as a bedtime and a wake-time picked from date pickers, not a single duration box. Wearable sleep can coexist with manual entries, and the app asks before overwriting one with the other. A source field keeps “apple-health” and “manual” honest in the record.

Heart rate, two ways in

Auto-synced from a wearable, or measured on the spot with a finger on the phone camera. The camera reading is awareness-level, not medical-grade, and the app says so.

A suggested goal, not a forced one.

Employees pick from four fitness goals. The app compares current weight to the ideal-weight range and highlights the goal that fits, but the choice stays theirs. Activity level, from Not Very to Very Active, feeds only the personal calorie math. It is never used to target people.

The HRA is optional. Skipping costs nothing.

The health risk assessment is a deeper, separate questionnaire. Finishing it returns a personal 0 to 100% fitness score, a status across seven bands from Critical to Excellent, and tips for the employee. It unlocks nothing and gates nothing: calorie targets come from the basic profile, and no one is penalized for skipping.

Private by design

Admins see company and department aggregates. No one in HR opens an individual’s blood pressure or weight history.

Before rollout

Is the camera heart-rate reading clinical?

No. It is an awareness-level reading taken with a finger on the phone camera, useful for a personal check-in. It is not medical-grade and we never present it as accurate or clinical.

Does the HRA affect calorie targets or anything else?

No. Calorie and macro targets calculate from the basic onboarding profile. The HRA returns a personal result for the employee and is not required for any other feature.

How is employee health data handled?

Individuals’ vitals stay private; HR sees aggregates only. The lab-report pipeline, a separate enterprise feature, follows HIPAA guidelines with BAA provisions. We do not claim platform-wide HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO, or GDPR certification.
