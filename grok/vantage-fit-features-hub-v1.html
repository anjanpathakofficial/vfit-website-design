Before you pick a card

Is the web app the same as the phone?

No. The full employee experience is iOS and Android. Web is steps, limited manual logging, and the admin dashboard. Lab-report upload works on web for enabled organizations.

What is Lite Mode?

Lite Mode is steps only. Fitness, nutrition, health metrics, mental extras, leagues, programs, and lab upload hide or collapse. In the builder, Lite keeps Race and step_count.

Who sees a lab report?

The employee sees the private plan. HR sees prevalence, not people. Health data upload is annual, whitelist, and select-partner. Hidden in Lite Mode.
