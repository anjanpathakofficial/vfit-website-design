How E-Marathon works

Steps become distance. Distance becomes the race.

Employees walk or run as they already do. Vantage Fit converts step counts into km or miles with a fixed company ratio, then ranks everyone on a distance leaderboard.

01

Steps sync

Phones (Apple Health / Google Fit) or optional wearables feed step counts automatically. No wearable required to join.

02

Convert

Default is 1,000 steps = 1 km. Admins can customize the ratio and choose km or miles for the leaderboard.

03

Rank by distance

The leaderboard shows total distance covered, not raw steps, so the marathon framing stays tangible and fair.

04

Finish the story

Share individual ranks and company-wide distance milestones so the whole workforce feels the finish line.

GPS
Distance comes from step count, not GPS.

Employees do not need routes, race bibs, or a special workout mode for regular daily steps to count toward the marathon. Walkers and runners use the same conversion rules.

Design the event

Multi-week distance campaign or one high-energy day.

E-Marathon duration is flexible. Match the format to the moment you need, then theme it like a real company race.

Multi-week

Steady accumulation

Best for 2–4 week flagship programs

Give people enough runway for meaningful distance and a visible leaderboard climb. Brand a finish line employees can talk about in the hallway and on the social feed.

  • Health months and quarterly campaigns
  • Collective company km goals
  • Simple setup: ratio, dates, audience
One-day

Shared company moment

Best for wellness days and re-engagement

A single 24-hour window creates urgency and same-day results. Promote about a week ahead, push on the morning of, and celebrate completions while energy is high.

  • World Heart Day, anniversaries, launches
  • Low commitment, high visibility
  • Pair with a longer challenge after kickoff

E-Marathons are intentionally simple. No weekly themes or multi-task scoring required.

    Create

    Choose E-Marathon. Name it like an event, set dates, add a marathon-themed banner.

Questions HR asks first

Fairness, distance, and event design.

Is distance tracked with GPS?
No for E-Marathon. Distance is calculated from step count using your company’s conversion ratio. Employees do not need GPS workouts or fixed routes. A separate ops-supported Marathon Event format exists for GPS-tracked physical races if you need that later. It is not the self-serve default.
Do walkers count the same as runners?
Yes. Regular daily steps convert to distance. Walking accumulates the same way running does under the fixed company ratio, so the event stays inclusive for non-runners.
How does the 1,000 steps = 1 km rule work?
That is the default conversion. Admins can change the ratio during setup and choose km or miles for the leaderboard. Everyone in the challenge uses the same ratio, so ranking stays consistent and transparent.
Should we run one day or several weeks?
Use one day for a shared company moment such as a wellness day, launch, or anniversary. Use about 2–4 weeks when you want meaningful cumulative distance and a longer leaderboard story. One-day events are not habit programs. Pair them with longer challenges if you need daily consistency after the spike.
Can global teams join one marathon?
Yes. Challenge start and end localize to each employee’s midnight. Audience filters can also scope by country or site if you prefer regional heat races under the same program brand.
