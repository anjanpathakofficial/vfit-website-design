Before you roll it out

Can HR create or stop a training plan?

No. Training Plans are VFit-managed. Admins cannot create, customize, or stop a plan. Only the employee can quit. HR can read an ongoing-programs report.

Do training plans issue certificates?

No. Training plans are personal, not competitive. Certificates belong to HR-run challenges.

Can we target content by activity level?

No. Content targeting uses demographics and health profile. Activity level is a personal calorie input on Health Metrics, not a targeting dimension.
