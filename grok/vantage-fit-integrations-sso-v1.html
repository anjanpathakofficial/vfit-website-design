Module 1 · Wearables

Phone first. Watch optional.

Steps come from Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android. A wearable is optional. Employees connect their own device. Wearable connection is standard and self-serve.

iOS default

Apple Health

Steps, workouts, weight, calories, sleep, heart rate, distance. Apple Watch flows automatically through a paired iPhone.

Android default

Google Fit

Default Android step source when no Fitbit or Garmin is connected. Samsung Watch is an Android device option.

Not real-time

Fitbit

OAuth. Steps, workouts, HR zones, sleep stages. Historical sync up to 30 days. Syncs on app open and on demand.

Webhook push

Garmin

OAuth 1.0a. Real-time webhook push, not periodic polling. Daily and activity summaries, GPS routes, 50 MB payloads. Tokens expire after 60 days.

SAML 2.0. Not a dashboard toggle.

Before you roll it out

Wearables

Do employees need a wearable?

No. The phone is enough. Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android cover steps. A watch adds accuracy and extra data types.

Is Fitbit real-time?

No. Fitbit syncs when the employee opens the app and on demand. Garmin is the real-time webhook integration. Garmin tokens expire after 60 days and need a reconnect.

SSO and HRIS

Can we turn SSO on from the admin dashboard?

No. SSO is SAML 2.0, set up by the integration team. It is not a self-serve toggle. HRIS readiness should be confirmed before a contract names a system.
