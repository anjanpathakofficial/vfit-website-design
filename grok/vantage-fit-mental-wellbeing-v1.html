Before you roll it out

Is this therapy or a clinical program?

No. It is a curated audio library. Completing a session logs a mindfulness activity. It is not therapy and not a diagnosis.

Does mood feed participation?

No. Minutes count. Mood, if the employee logs it, is a private perk. It is not scored and HR cannot see it.

Is the library available in Lite Mode?

Lite Mode is steps only. The mindfulness library extras are a Full-Mode surface. Additional sessions are requested through the account manager.
