Run, walk, cycle, wheelchair.

Corporate workout tracking software only earns trust if a car ride cannot look like a run.

  • Live GPS statsDistance, pace, duration, calories. Per-km or mile splits. Route map and pace graph. Optional social share. iOS Live Activity on Dynamic Island and Lock Screen.
  • iOS accuracy filterPoints under 100 m horizontal accuracy are dropped.
  • Android vehicle detectionFour methods: speed patterns, location consistency, high-pace share, split analysis. Minimum 10 m distance and 1 s gap.
  • Backend pace floorAverage pace under 3.5 min/km is rejected for running and walking. Each split must clear it too.
  • No cycling pace capCycle routes are mapped. Pace is not used as an anti-cheat.

Before you roll it out

Does GPS anti-cheat cap cycling pace?

No. The 3.5 min/km pace floor applies to running and walking, including each split. There is no cycling pace limit.

Do Apple Watch workouts import on their own?

No. iOS imports from Apple Health need a manual tap. Android imports use Health Connect. Duplicate workouts are blocked.

Can Lite Mode employees log a squat session?

No. Fitness logging is Full-Mode. Lite Mode is steps only.
