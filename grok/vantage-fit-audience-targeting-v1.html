Before you aim a challenge

Can I see who is in a health-risk cohort?

No. Health-risk targeting returns a count only. The list of users is not displayed to protect individual privacy. No screen or export exposes identities.

Do employees join themselves?

No. Employees do not self-join. An admin adds people by search or CSV, or an audience rule matches and enrolls them. Browse-and-join and QR join are not live.

What happens if someone is added late?

Leaderboard score counts from the join or add date forward. There is no retroactive credit or penalty.
