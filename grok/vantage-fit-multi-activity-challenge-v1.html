Design the program in the dashboard wizard.

Custom Challenge is self-serve — multi-week themes, mixed tasks, and scoring without a six-month build.

  1. 1
    Create

    Select Custom Challenge. Set name, dates, image, and public or private visibility.

  2. 2
    Theme weeks

    Name each week and set a theme logo and color so the program feels curated in the app.

  3. 3
    Add tasks

    Pick task types, targets, daily or weekly mode, and points. Drag to reorder within a week.

  4. 4

    Balance point values. Non-step tasks stop earning past 100% of target; steps use a daily cap (default 25,000).

    Invite, send mid-challenge notifications, and watch multi-task leaderboards and enrollment health.

Questions HR asks first

Straight answers before the demo.

How is this different from a step challenge?
Step challenges (Race, Streak, Journey, E-Marathon) center on steps or step-derived distance. Multi-activity uses the Custom Challenge format: multiple task types per week, weekly themes, and balanced multi-task scoring in one program.
Will desk workers and non-runners participate?
That is the design intent. Pair auto-tracked steps with water, mindfulness, content, adherence check-ins, and custom activities so people who are not high steppers can still complete tasks and earn points.
Is multi-activity available on every plan?
Full Mode includes all 27 task types. Lite Mode is Step Count only. Your account manager can confirm your mode and the upgrade path for multi-activity programs.
How does scoring work across different tasks?
Each task has its own point value. Employees earn toward the task target — non-step tasks do not pay extra past 100% of target; steps use a daily cap (default 25,000, adjustable with your account team). Total score sums points across tasks and weeks. Employees can view a score breakdown per task and week from the leaderboard.
Can we invent company-specific tasks?
Yes. Create custom loggable activities or adherence habits under Configuration → Activities, then add them as tasks in a Custom Challenge — for example Office Yoga, Walking Meetings, or Took Vitamins.
Do we have to build from scratch?
No. Use pre-built challenge templates with recommended tasks, targets, and weekly structure. Adjust dates, audience, branding, and difficulty before you publish.
