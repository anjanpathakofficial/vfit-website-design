Before you roll it out

Is this a HIPAA-compliant platform?

No. Vantage Fit is HIPAA-guideline aligned. BAA provisions apply to the lab-report pipeline. Lab reports are a different, whitelist page.

Must employees complete the HRA?

No. The HRA is optional. Skipping is allowed. Calorie targets use the basic health profile, not the HRA.

Can they log sleep on the web app?

No. Vitals, sleep, and the HRA are mobile-only and Full-Mode only.
