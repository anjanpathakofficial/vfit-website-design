BMR and goal

Science a buyer can check

Mifflin-St Jeor sets BMR. Harris-Benedict multiplies it. The goal then moves the daily number by 550 calories, about 0.5 kg a week. Male targets will not go below 1,800. Female targets will not go below 1,200.

  • Sedentary × 1.2
  • Light × 1.375
  • Moderate × 1.55
  • Heavy × 1.725
  • Very heavy × 1.9

Before you roll it out

Do employees need the full HRA for calorie targets?

No. Targets come from the basic onboarding health profile: height, weight, date of birth, gender, goal, and activity level. The full HRA is not required.

Can they log food on the web app?

No. Food and water logging are mobile-only. They are also absent in Lite Mode.

Is the food database comprehensive?

No. It is a few thousand items, with regional gaps. Manual entry and a food-suggestion path cover what search misses.
