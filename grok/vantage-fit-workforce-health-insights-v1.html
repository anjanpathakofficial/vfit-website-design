Org Wellness Score

One number. Four levers you can pull.

The Org Wellness Score is a weighted blend of health baseline and daily behavior. Diagnose which component moved, then design the next challenge or communication.

20% weight

Baseline

Health fundamentals from the Health Risk Assessment: BMI, blood pressure, chronic conditions, and lifestyle factors.

When weak: drive HRA completion and pair with annual check-up programs.
30% weight

Participation

Daily engagement. Are employees opening the app, logging activities, and joining challenges?

When weak: reminders, simpler entry challenges, broader audience rules.
30% weight

Activity

Physical effort and improvement over time: steps, workouts, calories, and personal trends.

When weak: Race or movement challenges that raise effort without excluding beginners.
20% weight

Adherence

Challenge compliance. Are people completing assigned tasks and maintaining habits they committed to?

When weak: Streaks, habit tasks, and clearer multi-week commitments.

A balanced profile is healthier than a high score driven by one pillar. Review monthly. Daily noise is not a strategy.

Questions HR asks first

Straight answers before the demo.

Is Workforce Health available on every plan out of the box?
Not as a silent self-serve toggle for every company. Your Vantage Fit account manager activates Workforce Health / Org Wellness Score for your organization. Once enabled, data begins accumulating immediately.
Can we use wellness scores for performance or promotion decisions?
No. Individual wellness scores are intended for aggregate program analysis and targeted interventions. Use the data to improve program design, not for HR decisions about specific employees.
Do we need the Health Risk Assessment for the score to work?
The score can run without HRA, but Baseline (20%) is less meaningful without it. Product defaults Baseline to a neutral value when HRA is missing. Enable HRA alongside Workforce Health for a fuller picture.
Is this three separate products for participation, challenges, and activity?
No. This Solutions page is one unified data-out story. Workforce Health combines those lenses into Org Score components, insights, and drill-downs. Standard Reports (enrollment, leaderboards, transactions) remain available for operational audits.
Can HR see individual lab values from health check-ups?
No. When lab upload is enabled, admins see aggregate, anonymized insights. Employees retain private access to their own reports and biomarker breakdowns.
