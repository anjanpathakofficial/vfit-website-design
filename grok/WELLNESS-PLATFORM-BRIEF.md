## Page job

Answer the search intent **“employee wellness platform”** with a clear product definition, not a second homepage and not a features mega-menu dump.

The page must make three things obvious in under a minute:

1. **What it is**: the daily employee wellness system (challenges + tracking + baseline + rewards + HR analytics).
2. **What it optimizes for**: participation and habit, not clinical surveillance.
3. **How the parts connect**: system story that routes buyers into Challenges, HRA, Insights, Rewards, and the hub.

**Buyer:** US enterprise HR / CHRO / Benefits / Wellbeing leaders. Program managers secondary. IT cares about trust and integrations.

**Primary CTA:** Book a demo (`https://www.vantagefit.io/request-demo/`)

## Research takeaways

### Product truth (from MISSION, FEATURE-INDEX, shared research, help/OS)

- **Public mission:** make employee wellbeing a daily habit, not a checkbox. Internal north star: whether employees open the app (adoption), not clinical data or cross-sell.
- **Habit product, not clinical:** behavior and engagement. Not telehealth, diagnostics, or surveillance. Employee privacy ahead of HR data hunger.
- **Platform surfaces that earn the category term:**
  - **Challenges library**: Race, Streak, Journey, E-Marathon, Custom; physical, mental, nutrition, sleep, team, holistic programs; self-serve + some ops-configured formats.
  - **Tracking**: steps and activity from phone or supported wearables (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin and related sources; sync varies by source).
  - **HRA / baseline**: voluntary in-app questionnaire; feeds **Baseline 20%** of Wellness Score; individual answers private; HR sees aggregate risk distribution.
  - **Workforce health insights**: Org Wellness Score 0–100 (Baseline 20% · Participation 30% · Activity 30% · Adherence 20%); participation/challenge/activity story; AI-streamed patterns; CSV export; aggregate privacy model.
  - **Rewards**: points for completed challenge tasks; wallet; gift-card redemption; optional unified balance with Vantage Circle R&R; marketplace is separate from points catalog.
  - **HR control**: launch/target audiences, manage programs, reports, SSO/HRIS/wearable integrations at enterprise level.
- **Compliance claims safe elsewhere on site:** HIPAA alignment, SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001 / 27701.
- **Search demand:** “wellness platform” ~850 is the largest Solutions term; page must earn the intent without restating the full homepage.

### What this page is *not*

- Not a clone of Invite → Participate → Reward → Measure flip-cards (homepage owns that narrative spine).
- Not a challenge program page (no formats explorer, no leaderboard integrity deep-dive).
- Not a feature matrix that competes with the Features mega-menu.
- Not a proof-heavy case-study page (optional proof omitted; platform is broader than any single customer story).

### Competitive framing (positioning, not claims)

Most “wellness platforms” are admin tools employees tolerate, or benefit portals that spike at launch and die by month two. Vantage Fit’s differentiation is **participation architecture**: inclusive challenges people can join without a wearable cult, rewards tied to real effort, a health baseline that stays private, and HR signals that improve the next program.

### FAQ

**What is an employee wellness platform?**  
The system an organization uses to deliver, manage, and measure wellbeing programs. Vantage Fit combines the employee daily experience with HR launch controls, rewards, and reporting.

**Do employees need a wearable?**  
No. Many programs work phone-first. Supported activity sources include common phone health platforms and wearables such as Fitbit and Garmin. Sync behavior depends on the source.

**Can HR see an employee’s personal health answers?**  
No. Individual HRA answers and personal health profiles stay with the employee. HR sees participation and aggregate health metrics for program design, not individual diagnosis or discipline.

**What does “participation” mean here?**  
Whether people open the app and complete qualifying wellness actions over time. The platform is built so that number is visible, trendable, and improvable with the next challenge or incentive.
