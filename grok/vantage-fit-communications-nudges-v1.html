What you send. What you request.

Preview, custom email, challenge notify, and publish push are self-serve. Master switch, per-type toggles, branding, and reminder timing go through your account manager. That is a process gate, not an annual-only tier.

Before you turn the emails on

Can I turn off one email type from the dashboard?

No. The master email switch and per-type toggles are account-manager-only today. You can preview templates and send a custom email yourself.

Do web users get push?

No. Push is iOS and Android, with deep links. Web-app users receive emails. Web push and in-app bell are not live.

Is Nudge Inactive Users automatic?

No. It is a rule-based Recommended Action. The send path is not specified. It is not a one-click send and it is not AI.
