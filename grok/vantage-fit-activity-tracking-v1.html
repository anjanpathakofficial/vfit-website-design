Two rings. Steps always.

The home screen opens on two rings. Ring 1 is always steps. Ring 2 is active calories or active minutes. VC Support sets which one.

With no challenge running, defaults are 10,000 steps and 250 active calories. Diary and 7-day trends sit under the rings. Mindful-minute and sleep graphs are Full-Mode only. Rings and trends are not on the web app.

65 types. Plus the ones you name.

The catalog lives in the log. Admins add the workplace-specific ones.

  • Custom loggableBranded items such as Office Yoga. Self-serve under Admin, Configuration, Activities.
  • Adherence activitiesHabit checks such as Avoid Sugar. Separate from the workout list.
  • WheelchairA GPS-trackable type, not a substitute for steps.
  • 7-Minute WorkoutA fixed 12-exercise routine with VFit-built GIFs.

Honest logs. Not inflated ones.

Integrity

Duplicate-detection and a 15-second floor

A push that is 95 to 105 percent of the prior day is ignored if it arrives within 18 hours. That rule targets a known iOS 1.5.4 to 1.5.7 cache bug. Step sync will not fire more often than every 15 seconds.

  • Step capping exists in code. It is off for every company.
  • One primary device at a time. Fitness covers the device catalog.

Before you roll it out

Do employees need a wearable?

No. Phone sensors are enough for steps. Wearables add accuracy and extra data types. They are not required.

Can HR open someone’s heart-rate sample?

No. Admins see aggregates. Not an individual’s heart-rate sample, GPS route, food log, or weight.

What disappears in Lite Mode?

Lite Mode is steps only. Custom activities, GPS, squat tracker, camera heart rate, food, water, and the second ring are Full-Mode.
