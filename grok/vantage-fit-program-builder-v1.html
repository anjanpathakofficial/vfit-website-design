Before you open the wizard

Can employees manage their own teams?

No. User-team creation and management is an ops toggle and is off by default. Team score is the average of member scores, not the sum.

Is a wearable required?

No. No format requires a wearable. Phone sensors and manual logs both count toward the same challenge.

Are Training Plans in this wizard?

No. Training Plans, Level, Marathon Event, and Weight Burn are ops-configured. They are not self-serve formats in this builder.
