How the program works

One Custom Challenge. Many wellbeing tasks.

Build a multi-week campaign with a theme each week. Assign mind-body, habit, and education tasks with points so the program feels curated, not like a pure steps race.

Illustrative 4-week theme (product task types)

Week 1

Move gently

  • Steps or active minutes
  • Yoga session log
Week 2

Steady mind

  • Meditation minutes
  • Mindfulness session
Week 3

Rest & recover

  • Sleep log
  • Guided sleep audio
Week 4

Daily habits

  • Water log
  • Adherence check-in
  • Content read

Themes and targets are configured by HR. Task mix is illustrative; all listed types exist in Custom Challenge (Full Mode).

Mind & body

Meditation & mindfulness

Log meditation minutes or complete guided sessions from a library of 30+ audio experiences across seven categories, including meditation, sleep, and relaxation.

Rest

Sleep tracking

Employees log bedtime and wake time, or sync from supported devices. Use sleep as a weekly task without turning rest into a public scoreboard of personal details.

Habits

Adherence & custom activities

Create yes/no daily habits and company-named activities (for example office yoga). Habits can outlast a single challenge and still earn points while the campaign runs.

Inclusive

Hydration & light movement

Water logs and optional steps keep programs open to every fitness level. Mix low-friction tasks so non-runners still finish the week.

Private

Mood check-in

Optional 1–5 self log with reason tags. Mood data is private to the employee, never shown to admins or on leaderboards.

Education

Content tasks

Award points for reading health articles or short-form wellness content so learning sits next to habits, not in a separate portal.

Full Mode unlocks multi-activity and mind-body tasks. Lite Mode is steps-only. Confirm mode with your account manager before promising a wellbeing mix.

Wellbeing programs stall when people fear surveillance. Vantage Fit separates program metrics from private personal logs.

01

Employees hide from individual rankings and still complete tasks, earn points, and contribute to team averages. Opt out is a Settings toggle, not a program exit.

Built for participation

Lower the stigma. Raise the join rate.

Wellbeing challenges work when they reward showing up, not only peak athletes on a steps race.

01

Low-barrier tasks

Five-minute meditation, water logs, and habit check-ins are easier to join than marathon-only culture. Daily or weekly targets keep goals realistic.

02

Consistency over athleticism

Non-step tasks cap at 100% of target so points reward completion, not over-grind. Recognition can celebrate streaks of showing up.

03

Optional teams

Team score averages individual contributions so departments support each other without forcing every person onto a public individual ladder.

04

Recognition that lands

Attach Vantage Points, optional certificates, and automatic badges so effort is visible beyond a podium photo of top finishers.

05

One app for mind and body

Guided mindfulness lives next to challenges and activity logs, with no second meditation login for employees to abandon after week one.

06

Private when needed

Opt out of leaderboards without leaving the program. Keep personal check-ins private while still counting toward team and program goals.

Questions HR asks first

Straight answers before the demo.

Is this a therapy or EAP product?
No. Vantage Fit is a wellbeing engagement platform for challenges, habits, and guided mindfulness content. It is not therapy, diagnosis, or a clinical treatment program. Pair it with your EAP or clinical partners as needed.
Can employees stay off the leaderboard?
Yes. A Settings toggle hides them from individual rankings. They still participate, complete tasks, earn points, and can contribute to team scores. Opting out does not remove them from the challenge.
What personal wellbeing data can HR see?
Participation and challenge standings. Admins cannot see individual mood logs, sleep patterns, food diary, weight, or Health Risk Assessment answers. Share that boundary with employees when you launch.
Do we need a separate meditation app?
No. Guided mindfulness audio (30+ sessions across seven categories such as meditation, sleep, and relaxation) ships in Vantage Fit and can count toward challenge tasks when you include mind-body activities.
What challenge format should we use for wellbeing?
Custom Challenge for multi-week, multi-activity themes (meditation, sleep, hydration, content, habits). Use Streak when you want a simple daily step habit. Race and distance formats fit short movement sprints and are available as related programs. Full Mode is required for mind-body task types.
