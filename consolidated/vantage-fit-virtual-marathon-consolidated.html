The problem
A parking-lot 5K was never going to include everyone. The runners sign up, everyone else watches, and participation is decided before it starts.
In one line
A virtual marathon turns everyday steps into race distance, so a full marathon is something the whole workforce chips away at over weeks, on foot, at their own pace, with no GPS run and no wearable required.

What is a corporate virtual marathon?−
A corporate virtual marathon is a distance event employees complete over a set period wherever they are, by turning their everyday steps into race distance. In Vantage Fit the self-serve E-Marathon counts 1,000 steps as 1 km by default, so walking gets people to the finish without a GPS run.
Do employees have to run, or use GPS?+
No. The step-based E-Marathon needs only steps from the phone, so walking counts and no wearable is required. A separate GPS Marathon Event exists for runners who want a timed, tracked race, and it is set up with your account manager.
How is distance counted?+
In the E-Marathon, steps convert to distance at a default of 1,000 steps per kilometre, which you can adjust. The GPS Marathon Event instead ranks by time per distance, so the fastest pace wins.
Does everyone need a wearable?+
No. Steps sync from the employee's phone. Wearables are optional and only add accuracy.
Can we run a real, timed marathon event too?+
Yes. The GPS Marathon Event offers 5K, 10K, half and full marathon tiers, tracked through the app's GPS workout. It is configured with your account manager rather than from the self-serve dashboard.
Is it fair for people who are not runners?+
That is the point. Distance comes from ordinary steps, badges reward reaching each milestone rather than speed, and anyone can opt out of the individual leaderboard while still finishing.
How long should the window be?+
Long enough that a walker can reach the target. Published Vantage Fit walkathons of this shape have run two weeks at POSOCO and 30 days at Landmark Group. A full 42.2 km at walking pace wants several weeks.
Do we have a marathon result we can quote?+
Not a marathon-specific one. The closest published Vantage Fit results are step-based walkathons: 97 percent participation at POSOCO over two weeks, and 5,000+ participants across six teams at Landmark Group.
