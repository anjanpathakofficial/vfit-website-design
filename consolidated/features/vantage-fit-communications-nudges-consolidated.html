Questions before rollout

Before you hit send

Everyday sends are self-serve. Branding, reminder timing and email toggles are set up with your account manager.

Audience targetingWorkforce health insights
What can I send without help?−
A custom branded email to all users, bulk challenge notifications and invitations, and push notifications with a preview and audience selection. You can also preview the lifecycle email templates.
Can I turn individual email types on or off myself?+
Today the master email switch and per-type toggles are handled by your account manager. Self-serve control over individual types is on the roadmap.
How does the inactive-user nudge work?+
A recommended action highlights anyone inactive for 30 days or more, so you can act on them. Exactly how that action sends is confirmed during setup.
Are messages personalized by AI?+
No. Messages are templated, branching on rules like timezone, language and branding. Nothing in the content is AI-generated.
Is there web-app or in-app bell notifications?+
Push goes to mobile, and the web app receives emails. In-app bell notifications and per-employee preferences are on the roadmap, not live today.
Which messages can HR send without help?+
Publishing push notifications, sending a custom branded email, previewing templates and notifying a challenge are all self-serve. Email toggles, the master switch, branding and reminder timing are changed through your account manager.
Do employees on the web app get push notifications?+
No. Web is email-only. Push is mobile, on iOS and Android, and deep-links straight to the right screen so a tap lands people where they need to act.
How many languages do the messages support?+
Notification content is available in 13 or more languages, so a global program reaches employees in the language they use the app in. Custom emails are written by HR in whichever language the audience needs.
