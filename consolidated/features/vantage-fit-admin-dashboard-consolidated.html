Questions HR asks in week one

Straight answers on the dashboard

Is the participation data real-time?−
Yes. The overview reflects live enrollment and activity, with deltas against the prior period and sparklines, filterable by date, country, department, age and gender.
Are the insights AI, and the recommended actions too?+
The leadership insights are AI-generated from aggregated signals with no PII sent to the model, and they are read-only. The recommended actions are rule-based shortcuts, up to five, not AI and not automations.
Can admins see an individual employee's health data?+
No. Employee reports show participation status only. Weight, BMI and assessment answers are never visible to an admin, and insights run on aggregated data.
What can we export for the board?+
Employee status, leaderboard, transaction and redemption reports to CSV, and completion certificates to PDF. League reports are available on annual plans.
What is included versus on an annual plan?+
The KPI overview, filters, At a Glance, recommended actions and the standard reports are included. Wellness Leagues and Health Insights are on annual plans, set up with your account manager. Health Insights is whitelist-gated, narrower than a plain annual plan.
Can the dashboard report across multiple countries?+
Yes. A multi-country org can read participation by unit or region, and data residency stays within the employee's own region (India, US, EU or UAE).
Do I need IT or a BI tool to export?+
No. The CSV export is one click inside the dashboard. Nothing needs to be provisioned, and no engineering ticket is required.
