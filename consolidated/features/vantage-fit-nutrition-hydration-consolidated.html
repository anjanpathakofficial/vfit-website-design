Questions before rollout

Worth asking before launch

Nutrition and hydration are employee-managed and always on for Full-Mode clients.

Activity trackingHealth metricsWellness challenges
Do employees need to finish the health assessment first?−
No. Targets come from the basic profile: height, weight, date of birth, gender, goal and activity level. The full health risk assessment stays optional.
Is the food database complete?+
Not yet, and there is no point pretending otherwise. It holds a few thousand items and is growing. When a food is missing, an employee types the name and its calories, so nothing blocks a log.
Can employees scan a barcode or photograph a meal?+
Not currently. Barcode scanning and photo logging are on the roadmap, not live features today.
Does it push extreme diets?+
No. A goal only shifts the target by up to 550 calories a day, and there are hard floors of 1,800 calories for men and 1,200 for women.
Does logging count toward challenges?+
Yes. A logged meal or glass of water feeds the participation score, and hydration can be set as a challenge task with its own target.
Does it work on the web?+
Food and water logging happen in the mobile app. They are part of Full Mode and are not included in the steps-only Lite Mode.
