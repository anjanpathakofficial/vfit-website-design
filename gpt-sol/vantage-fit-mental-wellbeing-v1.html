From audio to activity

Listen offline. Sync the completion later.

The employee meditation wellness program keeps the participation record intact even when the session plays without a connection.

  1. Choose a sessionBrowse the seven categories and download the MP3 from the curated library.
  2. Play online or offlineThe phone handles playback. A wearable is not part of the requirement.
  3. Complete and batch-syncVantage Fit records activity_id 1010 in minutes, with zero calories, when the connection returns.
  4. Count the participationThe logged minutes feed the same participation surface and eligible challenge tasks as other activity.
