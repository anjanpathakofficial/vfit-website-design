Questions before rollout

The practical answers your team will ask for.

Do employees need a smartwatch or fitness tracker?
No. A phone can provide step data through Apple Health on iPhone or the supported Android step path. Employees can also connect supported wearables such as Fitbit, Garmin, or Apple Watch.
Can we run a team challenge without favoring the largest team?
Yes, in formats that support teams. Team standings are based on average member scores rather than total team size. Race is intentionally an individual, pure-steps format; choose Streak, Journey, or a Custom challenge when team configuration is needed.
What can HR see about employees?
Admins can see participation status, challenge rankings, team scores, registration status, last-active date, and aggregate program measures. They cannot see individual weight, health assessment answers, lab results, food diaries, mood logs, or sleep records.
Can one challenge work across countries and time zones?
Yes. Challenge start, end, and daily reset are localized to each participant’s time zone, so every employee receives the same number of calendar days. Admin report date filters use the admin’s time zone.
What can we report after the challenge?
HR can export the filtered leaderboard to CSV with participant, department, score, step, team, and ranking fields. The dashboard also provides live step, score, and team leaderboard views.
