Questions before rollout

Questions before you form the teams

Bring your audience, timeline and team model to the demo. We will map them to the right challenge experience.

Are team scores totals or averages?
Team score is the average of member scores, not the sum. That keeps a larger roster from winning on headcount alone and makes each member’s contribution matter.
Can employees create their own teams?
Yes, when employee team creation is enabled for your company. A captain can create a team and invite colleagues. HR can also pre-create teams, bulk-upload them and manage membership from the dashboard.
Can someone participate without appearing on the leaderboard?
Yes. An employee can opt out of individual leaderboard visibility and continue participating, earning points and contributing to the team average.
Which challenge formats work with teams?
Use a supported team format that fits the behavior you want to build. Custom, Streak and Journey experiences can support team configuration. Race is a pure individual steps leaderboard. We will confirm the best fit for your company setup during the demo.
What can HR report after the challenge?
HR can export leaderboard data to CSV, including participant, department, score, rank, step and team fields where applicable. The live dashboard also provides score, step and team views.
