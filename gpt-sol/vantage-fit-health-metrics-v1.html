The participation connection

One update. One activity entry.

The employee health metrics tracking platform stays connected to everyday participation rather than becoming a separate questionnaire portal.

01

Sleep starts with the clock

Employees choose bedtime and wake-time, not just a duration. A manual override is available. If wearable sleep already exists, Vantage Fit asks before replacing it and records the source as manual or apple-health.

Full-Mode adds the sleep trend graph.
02

Heart rate keeps its limits

A reading can sync from a supported wearable or use a finger on the phone camera. Camera heart rate is awareness-level, not medical-grade. It is not presented as diagnosis or clinical monitoring.

No wearable is required for the camera option.
03

Weight and vitals join the log

Employees can use a scroll picker or a wearable for weight. It is stored in grams and shown in the employee’s unit. Blood pressure accepts systolic and diastolic values, including 120/80. Cholesterol and diabetes status can sit in the same private profile.

Each update creates the activity log automatically.

Optional deeper assessment

A personal result, not an HR score.

The Health Risk Assessment is a separate, skippable questionnaire for employees who want a deeper view.

  • 0–100% personal fitness resultSeven bands run from Critical through Excellent, with personalized tips.
  • Optional from start to finishEmployees can skip it without penalty. It is not required for calorie targets.
  • About 20% completionObserved in one cohort. [VERIFY]

HRA answers are personal. The assessment is absent from Lite Mode and does not create a web-app logging surface.

Profile guidance

Guidance that suggests. Never restricts.

BMI bands and an ideal-weight range help highlight a sensible goal. The employee can still choose any available goal.
