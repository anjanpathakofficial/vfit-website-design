# Remaining Solutions set notes

## Shipped set

The six pages were delivered in the signed-off Solutions IA order:

1. **Wellness challenges**: a flagship library and goal-first chooser, with accessible program filtering and format guidance.
2. **Health Risk Assessment**: a focused data-in page built around the employee-to-HR privacy boundary.
3. **Workforce health insights**: a data-out page that keeps participation, challenge, activity, and aggregate health-risk signals independent.
4. **Wellness rewards program**: an action page that follows effort through points, wallet visibility, gift-card redemption, and reporting.
5. **Wellness platform**: a category-definition page organized around the employee and HR participation loop.
6. **Solutions hub**: a lightweight route map that mirrors the two-column mega-menu and the data in to data out to action sequence.

Each page has a strategy brief and a high-fidelity HTML mock in this folder.

## Cross-page design principles

- **One system, six jobs:** Shared Noto Sans typography, Vantage Fit coral, mint, ink, canvas, navigation behavior, button language, radii, shadows, footer treatment, and responsive conventions keep the set coherent. Each page uses a different information architecture suited to its role.
- **Participation is the spine:** Every page connects its product job to an honest participation outcome without turning wellness into surveillance or a clinical claim.
- **Product UI over abstract decoration:** Page signatures come from real product concepts: a program shelf, privacy boundary, intelligence rail, transaction receipt, participation loop, and route map.
- **Separate health signals stay separate:** HRA is the voluntary baseline, insights is the aggregate interpretation layer, and rewards is the action layer. The mocks do not collapse unlike measures into a composite score.
- **Privacy is specific:** Individual HRA answers, lab reports, and biomarker values remain outside the HR view. Admin health-risk stories are aggregate and retain assessed-population context.
- **Proof stays grounded:** No customer statistics, compliance badges, catalog sizes, benchmarks, or outcome claims were invented. Illustrative product views are labeled or avoid measured-looking values.
- **Conversion stays consistent:** Book a demo is the primary CTA and See pricing is the secondary CTA. Sibling routes use local filenames so the prototype set works from disk.

## Quality checks

- All 6 briefs and all 6 HTML mocks exist with the required filenames.
- `html-validate` passes across the complete set.
- Every page has one title, meta description, H1, and main landmark.
- Local sibling links, fragment links, stylesheet links, and image paths resolve.
- IDs are unique and all image elements include alt text.
- Visible focus treatment, responsive breakpoints, and reduced-motion handling are present on every page.
- Copy scans pass for em dashes, mailto demo CTAs, and unsupported metrics.
- The required browser preview backend was unavailable, so rendered screenshot QA could not be completed in this environment. Source, semantic, responsive-rule, and link validation are complete.
