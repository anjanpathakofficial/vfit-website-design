# Vantage Fit wellness platform

## Page job

Define Vantage Fit for a buyer searching for an employee wellness platform, then make the operating model easy to understand. This page should answer three questions quickly: what the platform is, how employees participate, and how HR runs and improves the program. It should not repeat the homepage's full feature inventory.

**Audience:** US enterprise HR, benefits, wellbeing and program leaders. IT and privacy reviewers are a secondary audience.

**Intended URL:** `/solutions/wellness-platform/`

**Primary conversion:** Book a demo

**Secondary conversion:** See pricing

## Product truth used

- Vantage Fit's public mission is to make employee wellbeing a daily habit, not a checkbox. Internal positioning makes adoption the north star and explicitly describes Vantage Fit as a habit and engagement product, not a clinical or surveillance product.
- Employees can join challenges, track activity and health habits, and earn points. Challenge completion, admin awards, wallet transactions, and gift card redemption are documented product paths.
- Steps can sync from Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, and Garmin. Sync behavior varies by source, so the page says “connect supported sources” rather than promising identical real-time behavior for every device.
- HR can create and schedule challenges, target audiences, manage participants, monitor participation, review challenge and activity performance, and export filtered reports.
- HRA and health information have a different privacy boundary from program participation. Employees see their personal health detail, while admins see aggregate health metrics rather than individual HRA answers, lab results, or personal health profiles.
- The current admin information model treats participation, challenge performance, activity trends, and health risk as separate measures. The page does not invent a single composite impact score.

## Messaging decision

The category definition is:

> Vantage Fit is an employee wellness platform that helps HR launch inclusive wellbeing programs, helps employees participate through everyday activity and challenges, and gives program leaders clear signals to improve what they run next.

That definition becomes a three-part system instead of a feature dump:

1. **Give employees a reason to return.** Accessible activity tracking, varied challenge formats, progress, teams and rewards create repeat participation.
2. **Give HR one place to operate.** Plan, target, launch, monitor and report without stitching together separate campaign tools.
3. **Keep the feedback loop useful.** Participation, challenge, activity and aggregate health-risk signals inform the next program and the next incentive.

The platform does not claim to diagnose, treat, guarantee outcomes, or expose individual health detail to an employer.

## Page architecture

1. **Hero and plain-language definition**
   - Search-intent H1: “The employee wellness platform built for participation”
   - A compact product visual connects employee actions to HR decisions.
   - CTA pair: Book a demo / See pricing.
2. **Category definition strip**
   - “One employee experience. One HR operating view. One measurable participation loop.”
   - Clarifies that the product is a shared system, not a menu of unrelated benefits.
3. **Two-sided product experience**
   - Employee side: connect, choose, join, progress, earn.
   - HR side: plan, target, launch, understand, improve.
   - Product-real UI cards are shown rather than abstract wellness illustration.
4. **Participation loop**
   - Launch a relevant program → make participation easy → understand response → reinforce useful habits.
   - This is the page's signature element and organizing model.
5. **Data boundaries built into the model**
   - Clearly separates personal employee health detail from HR's aggregate program view.
6. **Choose a solution**
   - Routes to Wellness challenges, Health Risk Assessment, Workforce health insights, and Wellness rewards program.
7. **Focused FAQ**
   - Platform definition, devices, health-data visibility, and reporting.
8. **Conversion close**
   - “Build a wellness program people can take part in.”

## Visual direction

The page inherits the enterprise system's Noto Sans typography, coral action color, ink, mint, soft gray and dark surfaces. Its distinct signature is a **participation loop**: a product-shaped circuit that moves from HR launch controls into an employee challenge card, then back into separate program signals. The circuit encodes the real operating model and avoids the generic SaaS “feature grid plus dashboard screenshot” treatment.

The hero is bright and direct for category clarity. The operating loop section switches to the dark enterprise surface and uses mint as the signal color. Motion is limited to a slow signal pulse and card lift, both disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### FAQ

**What is an employee wellness platform?**

It is the system an organization uses to deliver, manage and measure employee wellbeing programs. Vantage Fit combines the employee participation experience with HR program controls and reporting.

**Do employees need a specific wearable?**

No. Vantage Fit supports phone-based activity sources and connected services including Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit and Garmin. Sync behavior depends on the source and device settings.

**Can HR see an employee's personal health information?**

HR can see program activity and aggregate health metrics. Individual HRA answers, lab results and personal health profiles remain visible to the employee rather than the employer.

**What can HR report on?**

Available reporting includes enrollment and participation, challenge leaderboards, activity transactions, points and redemptions, plus configured workforce-health views. Reports can be filtered and exported as CSV.
