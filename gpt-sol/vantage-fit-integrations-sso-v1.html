Module A · Wearables and device sync

Device activity, without a device mandate.

A phone is enough for steps. Optional connections add richer activity while one primary source and trusted-source filtering keep the participation number coherent.

Phone first

Self-serve

Apple Health on iPhone and Google Fit on Android provide steps. Apple Health also brings workouts, weight, calories, sleep, heart rate, and distance. Apple Watch follows its paired iPhone automatically.

Fitbit

Not real-time

OAuth sync covers steps, workouts, heart-rate zones, sleep stages, and up to 30 days of history. Refresh happens on app open or on demand.

Garmin

Real-time webhook

The three-step OAuth 1.0a flow is the only real-time device connection. It supports daily and activity summaries, GPS routes, historical backfill, and payloads up to 50 MB. Tokens expire after 60 days.

More supported paths

Optional

Samsung Watch is an Android device option. More than 70 trusted third-party apps can write through Apple Health, including Zepp, Mi Band, Huawei, Nike Run Club, and Strava.

One source reaches one participation surface.

Source ruleOne primary device

Fitbit or Garmin replaces the phone source to avoid double-counting.

Trust ruleFilter the source

Trusted step-source filtering is active. No active daily cap is claimed.

OutcomeCount the same way

A phone-only day or Garmin run can land in the same challenges and participation number.
