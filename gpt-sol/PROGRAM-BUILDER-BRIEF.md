# Program Builder & Templates

## Page job

Prove that an HR program manager can launch the participation surface before lunch without needing a services project. The page is a product capability page, not a challenge-program landing page.

## Research takeaways

- The self-serve wizard supports five formats: Custom, Race, Journey, Streak, and E-Marathon. Drag-and-drop task configuration belongs to Custom only.
- Custom can use 27 task types. Mood can be a private employee log, but HR does not see mood answers.
- The only named Journey templates are Backpacking through Europe and Journey to 7 Wonders. Use “2+ built-in journey templates.” Europe has nine stations and reaches 70,000 steps, approximately 50 km. This is illustrative.
- Shared setup covers name, description, image, dates, privacy, target audience, teams, points, and certificates.
- Campaign management uses six states plus admin controls to reschedule, force-start today, or end today without points. There is no calendar UI.
- Lite Mode is Race plus step count and can deploy in 1–2 days.
- Team scores are averages. Monetary point rewards are account-manager-gated and live for one company. Level, Marathon Event, Weight Burn, Training Plans, custom scoring, and employee team creation are not self-serve wizard formats.

## Structure and visual direction

1. Split hero: a concise “before lunch” promise, required product screenshot, and workplace photograph.
2. Five-format rail: the decision an admin makes first, with Custom visually expanded to reveal drag-and-drop.
3. Product proof: second required campaign-management screenshot beside a compact 27-task drawer.
4. Journey proof: only the two named routes and the illustrative Europe distance.
5. Lifecycle strip: six literal system states and three admin overrides, deliberately not a calendar.
6. Guardrails: Lite Mode, average team scoring, certificates, gated rewards, and ops-only formats.
7. CTA and links to the other HR capabilities.

The visual system stays inside Vantage Fit’s Noto Sans, ink, coral, mint, canvas, 22px radii, existing nav, and existing footer. The signature element is the operational launch rail. It encodes the actual creation sequence and keeps the page from reading like a generic challenge catalogue.

## Critic result

Pass. The page does not use Org Wellness Score, a calendar, a third named Journey, summed team scores, or freely available monetary rewards. It distinguishes Custom drag-and-drop from the other formats, labels the Europe proof illustrative, keeps ops-only formats out of the wizard, uses both required CDN product shots and a real photograph, links `enterprise.css`, and stays within the requested copy budget.
