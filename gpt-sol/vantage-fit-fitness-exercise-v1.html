GPS workout tracking

Four workouts get the route.

Run
Walk
Cycle
Wheelchair

Mobile GPS shows live distance, pace, duration, calories, per-kilometre or per-mile splits, a route map, and a pace graph. Employees can optionally share a result. On iOS, Live Activity can keep progress visible on the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen.

GPS integrity, stated precisely.

Controls differ by platform and activity type.

iOS

Filters GPS points with less than 100 m horizontal accuracy.

Android

Checks speed patterns, location consistency, high-pace percentage, and split behaviour. Points require 10 m distance and a 1 second gap.

Run + walk

The backend rejects averages faster than 3.5 min/km. Every split must clear the same threshold.

Cycle

No cycling pace limit exists, so none is implied.

Workout imports

Import by choice. Prevent duplicates.

Supported imports land in the same diary and participation record.

iOS

Apple Health requires a tap

Employees manually choose the workout to import. The import is not automatic.

Android

Health Connect handles imports

Supported workouts import through Health Connect. Duplicate IDs and matching sessions are blocked.

Wearable integration

Connect the device an employee already uses.

A wearable is optional. When employees bring one, Vantage Fit keeps the source rules clear.

Full Mode only. Fitness tools are unavailable in Lite Mode, which is steps only. Employees connect Fitbit and Garmin themselves. HR sees aggregate connection statistics, not an individual's route. Simultaneous-activity prevention and Lite Mode changes require VC Support.
