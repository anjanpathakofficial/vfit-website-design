# Mental Wellbeing and Mindfulness page brief

## Page job

Prove that Vantage Fit’s guided mindfulness library is part of the same employee participation system as movement. The page is for HR and benefits leaders who need to see exactly how a completed session becomes a challenge-ready activity.

**URL:** `/features/mental-wellbeing/`  
**Primary keyword:** corporate mindfulness meditation app  
**Secondary keyword:** employee meditation wellness program

## Core message

**Meditation minutes count as participation.** Employees choose from a fixed, curated library, listen online or offline, and finish a session. Vantage Fit records the completed minutes as mindfulness `activity_id 1010`, then makes that activity available to participation reporting and challenge tasks.

Mood is not part of this story. The page does not position a private check-in as an HR metric.

## Page structure

1. Hero: a session player, a compact completion receipt, and the direct participation claim.
2. Curated library: 30+ sessions across seven named categories with a 1–34 minute range.
3. Completion path: download, play offline, sync later, and log `activity_id 1010` in minutes with zero calories.
4. Challenge task proof: show how “complete a mindfulness session” sits beside other challenge tasks.
5. Global delivery and scope: 13+ languages, tailored additions through an account manager, and the non-clinical boundary.
6. Assigned program proof, related pages, and one CTA band.

## Visual direction

Use the shared Vantage Fit system from `styles/enterprise.css`: Noto Sans, ink, coral, mint, canvas, rounded cards, nav, buttons, and footer. The hero remains a light enterprise SaaS composition rather than a dark meditation scene.

The signature element is a “completed session” receipt with a visible path from 12 minutes to `activity_id 1010` to challenge participation. A horizontal category rail behaves like a compact audio shelf. The real session-player and mental-wellbeing product shots carry the interface proof, while the people photograph shows a plausible workday reset.

The first concept used a large breathing orb. It was removed because it could belong to a consumer meditation app and would hide the product mechanic. The revised receipt is specific to Vantage Fit’s participation model.

## Facts to show

- The curated catalog contains 30+ sessions across Top Picks, Meditation, Yoga, Relaxation, Sleep, White Noise, and Self-Awareness.
- Sessions run from about 1 to 34 minutes.
- MP3 sessions can play offline and batch-sync later.
- Completion logs mindfulness `activity_id 1010`, unit minute, with zero calories.
- “Complete a mindfulness session” can be assigned as a challenge task.
- Content is localized in 13+ languages.
- Select organizations can request additional or tailored sessions through their account manager.

## Proof and gates

Use Wipro Wellbeing Fest’s 1,980 mindfulness minutes with `[VERIFY]`. Do not add unrelated mental-health task figures. Core library, offline play, later sync, activity logging, and challenge-task use are standard. Additional or tailored sessions are account-manager or select-partner work.

State that this is a guided content library, not therapy. Do not describe recommendations as AI, the catalog as unlimited, or a wearable as required.
