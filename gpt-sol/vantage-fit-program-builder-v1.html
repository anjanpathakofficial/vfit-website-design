Scope before setup

Know what is self-serve before you plan.

The builder is broad, but the boundaries are explicit. That keeps the program brief realistic before configuration starts.

Self-serve

Five formats and certificates

Configure challenge branding, certificate logo, seal, signature image, signer details, and description. Team scores use the average of member scores.

Lite Mode

Race plus step count

Use a steps-only rollout for one-time campaigns or stricter data environments. Deployment can take 1–2 days.

Account manager

Monetary point rewards

Rewards are gated and live for one company today. They are not a freely enabled builder switch.

Ops-only

Specialized formats stay outside this wizard

Level, Marathon Event, Weight Burn, Training Plans, custom scoring, and employee-managed teams require operational setup.
