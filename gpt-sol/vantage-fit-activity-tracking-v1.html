Employee activity tracking that counts every valid move.

One logging surface

Three ways in. One participation record.

Different inputs resolve into the same diary, challenge logic, and aggregate participation reporting.

Device sync

Apple Health on iPhone, Google Fit or the selected Android source, plus direct Fitbit and Garmin connections.

Manual logs

Employees record activities a phone cannot infer. Manual steps are available when the company toggle is enabled.

Camera tracking

Squat pose detection counts movement. Finger-on-camera heart rate is awareness-level, not medical-grade.

Participation

Each valid log can count toward the same challenge and participation number.

Health Connect supports selected Android imports, not the live Android step pipeline. HR sees aggregates, not an employee's route or health sample.

Active minutes

100 steps. One active minute.

100steps = 1 active minute

Total steps can combine device steps, company-enabled manual steps, and steps derived from active minutes. The move_min challenge task uses the same mechanic.

  • Ring one always shows steps.
  • Ring two shows active calories or active minutes, configured through VC Support.
  • Default no-challenge targets are 10,000 steps and 250 active calories.
  • Employees can open the diary and seven-day trends on mobile. Mindful-minute and sleep graphs are Full Mode only.
  • The WHO 150-minute weekly recommendation is context for active minutes, not a certification.

Data integrity

Measured, not inflated.

The system addresses common duplication patterns without making claims the product cannot support.

15 seconds

Minimum interval between step sync requests controls repeated reads and battery use.

Duplicate check

A narrow 95 to 105 percent prior-day pattern within 18 hours targets specific older iOS builds.

One source

Fitbit or Garmin replaces Apple Health or Google Fit as the primary step source, preventing double-counting.

No cap claim

Step capping exists in code but is disabled globally, so it is not presented as active protection.

Access boundaries

Full Mode

The complete activity logging surface, including custom activities and rings beyond steps.

Lite Mode

Steps only. Custom activities, GPS, camera tools, and rings beyond steps are unavailable.

Admin self-serve

Create custom loggable or adherence activities and use activity reports.

VC Support

Enable manual step entry, the active-minutes ring, overlapping-activity prevention, or Lite Mode.
