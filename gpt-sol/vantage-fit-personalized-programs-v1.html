Employee enrollment

Three answers shape the week.

The employee controls the setup, previews the schedule, and starts a personal challenge the following Monday.

  1. Choose 4–8 weeksA slider sets the plan length.
  2. Choose 3–6 training daysA radio choice sets weekly frequency.
  3. Choose the daysCheckboxes place training and rest days.
  4. Preview, then Start ChallengeThe week-by-week plan starts next Monday inside the Challenges tab.
Personal challenge rules

The employee owns the stop button.

  • Employee: can start, follow and quit the plan.
  • HR: cannot stop, create, modify or disable an individual plan.
  • Admin view: read-only ongoing-program reporting.
  • Completion: no certificate because the plan is personal and non-competitive.
