Not a dimension: activity level. Targeting does not infer an audience from who logged more or less.

Health-risk boundary

Health-risk targeting ends at the count.

Workforce Health can match defined risk-code categories such as diabetes, heart, liver, kidney, thyroid, blood health, Vitamin D or B12 deficiency, and inflammation. It does not expose arbitrary biomarker thresholds or the people behind them.

Enrollment mechanics

Two enrollment paths. No accept step.

Add known participants directly or let an audience rule enroll every match. Both routes put the challenge in the employee app without relying on a legacy email invite flow.

Admin add

Choose the employees you already know

Use search for an individual or upload a CSV for a larger group.

  • Search by employee record
  • Bulk add by CSV
  • Enrollment is immediate
Rule enroll

Enroll every matching employee

Save the audience rule and let matching employees enter instantly.

  • Rules use approved dimensions
  • New matches enroll automatically
  • Challenge remains private
Challenge startsEmployee joinsScore starts here

Late joiners score from their join or add date forward. There is no retroactive credit and no penalty for the period before enrollment.

Campaign context

The challenge stays private by default.

Audience rules live inside challenge creation and management. Applying any targeting filter automatically changes visibility to Private.
