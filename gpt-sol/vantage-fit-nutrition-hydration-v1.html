An employee nutrition tracking app built on clear math.

Calorie targets

Start with BMR, not a database count.

The daily target follows a visible calculation and keeps the full HRA out of the critical path.

Corporate wellness food logging

Search fast. Enter manually when search misses.

Choose the mealBreakfast, lunch, snacks, or dinner sets the available target.
Search or open Quick TrayAlgolia finds listed foods. Frequent foods return in Quick Tray.
Set serving and quantityReview calories, protein, carbs, and fat before saving.
Save to participationThe meal lands in the same activity diary and challenge surface.

The database contains a few thousand items and regional coverage is still improving. Manual entry accepts a food name, calories, and macros without database dependency. Employees can also submit a food suggestion.

Employee hydration tracking

One water tap is always 250 ml.

The plus or minus control changes intake by one fixed glass. Employees can also enter an exact amount in litres or fluid ounces.

250 mlFixed size of one glass
8 glassesDefault daily target
CustomChallenge targets, such as 3.0 L on four days

Full Mode, mobile, and private. Food and water are absent in Lite Mode and are not available on the web. Employees manage their own entries. HR sees aggregate participation, not an individual's food diary.
