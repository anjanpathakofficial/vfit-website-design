The two that get overstated

Sleep is a bedtime and a wake time.

Not a duration box to estimate into. And a phone-camera pulse reading is an awareness number, not a clinical one.

Sleep Two date pickers
Bedtime11:20 pm
Wake time6:35 am
A connected wearable's sleep coexists with manual entries and asks before it overwrites one. Each record keeps its source, so an Apple Health night and a typed night are never confused. The sleep trend graph is a Full Mode surface.
Manual override stays open on any night the wearable missed.
Heart rate Camera or wearable
Auto-synced from a connected wearable, continuously and during workouts. Or a fingertip held over the rear camera for 15 to 30 seconds, with no device at all. The result is a resting BPM reading the employee keeps.
Two minutes seated first gives a usable resting number.
The camera reading is awareness-level, not medical-grade.

It is a convenience for an employee without a wearable, and it is not a diagnostic. We do not chart it as a clinical trend. For continuous or exercise heart rate, connect a device.

Participation

The health update is another log, not a second product.

A vitals or weight update creates an activity-log entry on its own. The fuller health picture lands in the same diary as a step, against the same participation rate.

No separate form, no second submission, nothing for HR to reconcile. On Android, Health Connect imports weight, sleep, heart rate, calories and distance into the same diary.

Goals

Recommended, never enforced.

The app compares current weight to the ideal range and highlights one goal. All four stay selectable.

Lose weight Reduce belly fat Gain muscle Be fitter

Activity level, from Not Very Active to Very Active, is a personal calorie input. It is not an HR targeting dimension.

Optional and deeper

Skipping the HRA costs nothing.

A longer questionnaire across nine health areas returns a personal fitness score from 0 to 100% and a status band. Nothing else on this page waits on it.

Blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, waist, smoking, alcohol, exercise, blood group, age and BMI. The result sits on a seven-band status ladder from Critical to Excellent, with tips against whatever scored low. Calorie targets use the basic profile, not the HRA. No feature is gated behind finishing it. Retake it whenever something changes.
The HRA is not available in Lite Mode, and neither are vitals, sleep or My Health. Lite Mode is steps only.

Rollout questions

Three things buyers ask in week one.

Does anyone at my company see my blood pressure?

No. Admins see aggregate counts, such as how many people completed the HRA. Individual vitals, readings, bands and answers are never exposed.

Is the HRA required to get calorie targets?

No. Targets come from the basic health profile: height, weight, age and gender. The HRA is separate and optional, and no other feature depends on it.

Can employees do any of this on the web?

No. Vitals, sleep, heart rate and the HRA are mobile only. Lab-report upload is the one health surface that works on the web.
