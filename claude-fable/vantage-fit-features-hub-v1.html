The employee app, where the full logging surface lives.

Three questions the grid does not answer.

Does everything work in a browser?

No. Employees get the full logging surface on iOS and Android. On the web they get steps and limited manual logging. HR gets the whole admin dashboard in a browser. Uploading a lab report is the one employee action that is built for the web.

What changes in Lite Mode?

The logging surface collapses to steps. In the program builder you get the Race format and the step count task, and nothing else. Fitness, nutrition, health metrics, the mental wellbeing extras, leagues, personalized programs and health data upload are all off.

Who gets to read an uploaded lab report?

The employee, and nobody in HR. An upload returns a private personal plan to that employee. Admins see prevalence counts for a cohort, never a named report. The pipeline is annual and whitelisted, and it carries BAA provisions for HIPAA compliance.
