An employee uploads from iOS, Android or the web. PDF up to 30 MB, or a JPG or PNG that gets converted to PDF first.

1 Extract

Text and values are read out of the report.

2 Match

Test names are corrected against a biomarker database.

3 Insight

Values are compared with reference ranges.

4 Summary

The report becomes a readable visual summary.

5 Suggestions

General wellness guidance, drawn from the flagged areas.

Extraction is the AI step. The suggestions that follow are general wellness guidance and not medical advice. The disclaimer stays on the screen, and an out-of-range value is a reason to talk to a doctor.

Before you scope it

Can our HR team see an employee's biomarker values?

No. Extracted values belong to the employee. HR sees org-level prevalence per health area, and there is no export that reveals a person.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Suggestions are general wellness guidance, not medical advice. The disclaimer stays on screen, and an out-of-range value is a reason to talk to a doctor.

Can we upload our diagnostic vendor's reports in bulk?

Bulk upload over SFTP is running for one company today. Ask your account manager rather than planning around it.
