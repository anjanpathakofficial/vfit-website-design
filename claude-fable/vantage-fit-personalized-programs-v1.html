Health BitesStep-through micro-lessons, 2 to 3 minutes

Content as a challenge task.

Watch this video, read this article, complete bite-size content. Each completes itself when the item is opened or stepped through, and a finished Health Bite logs an activity.

Quizzes are optional and auto-evaluated. Every answer has to be right to pass, and a pass or a fail both still log. A Health Bite is images, text and quiz screens in sequence, in more than one language. Targeting is by age, gender and health profile.

Activity level is not a targeting dimension. It is a personal calorie input on health metrics and nothing reads it here.

Rollout questions

Three things buyers ask in week one.

Can our admins build their own training plans?

Not today. The catalog is curated by Vantage Fit, and admins cannot create, edit or disable individual plans. What admins build is challenges, a separate system.

Are the plans AI-generated?

No. The progression is rule-based, and the three answers given at enrollment shape the schedule.

What happens if someone quits halfway?

The plan stops, and starting again means starting from scratch rather than resuming. Everything logged along the way still counts toward participation.
