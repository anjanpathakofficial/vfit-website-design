Reminders are off until you ask

Challenge Reminder ships disabled, because a nudge every week of a long program reads as noise. Turn it on for the challenge where participation is slipping, not for all of them.

What you send today, and what your account manager sets.

Sending is self-serve. Configuring what the system sends on its own is not, for any customer.

In the dashboard now

You send it

Four things, all live, all without a ticket.

  • Preview emailsConfiguration, Preview Emails. See each template with your branding on it.
  • Send a custom emailCommunity, Send Custom Email. Subject, body, attachments, template selection.
  • Notify or invite a challengeManage Challenge, Notify or Invite. Goes to that challenge's participants.
  • Publish a push notificationCommunity, Publish Notifications. Write, choose the audience, preview, send.
Account manager

They configure it

A process gate for every customer, not a contract tier you can buy out of.

  • Per-type email togglesTurning Weekly Summary off, or Challenge Reminder on.
  • The master email switchSilences all Vantage Fit email when you run comms yourself.
  • Banner, logo, colours, program nameApplied per company. Deeper copy changes are mediated too.
  • Reminder timing24, 48 or 72 hours before a challenge starts.

The inactive-user shortcut is a rule, not a robot.

The dashboard surfaces up to five Recommended Actions, ordered by priority. Two of them are about the people who stopped logging.

1
View Inactive Employees

Opens the Employee Report filtered to people with no activity in the last 30 days.

2
Nudge Inactive Users

A rule-based shortcut into the admin screen for reaching that group. It is not a confirmed one-click send.

Send path: verify
3
Check Health Insights

Only appears when that surface is enabled on your account.

Rule-based, and templated

Recommended Actions are triggered by thresholds in your own data. Email and push copy comes from templates. Neither is AI-written, and neither picks a segment out of a report for you.

Recommended Actions sit on this dashboard, next to the participation number they are reacting to. Figures inside the screenshot are sample data.
