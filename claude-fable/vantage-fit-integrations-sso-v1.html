01 Wearables and device sync Employees and HR

Wearables, and the phone in every pocket.

Steps come from Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android before anyone buys anything. A tracker adds detail, not access.

Apple Health

Connecting a device in the employee app.

One primary device at a time

Connect Fitbit or Garmin and it becomes the step source. Apple Health and Google Fit stop feeding steps, so no day is counted twice.

Recognised sources only

Steps arrive from known apps and devices. Unrecognised sources are rejected.

Wearables questions

Does everyone need a tracker before we launch?

No. Phone sensors cover steps on both platforms. Most companies launch first and let people connect a device later.

Which connection updates in real time?

Garmin, through webhook push. Fitbit syncs when the app opens and on demand, so treat it as near-live.

Devices settle what gets logged. Identity settles who can log at all.

Both halves exist for one reason: a Garmin run and a phone-only step day land on the same participation number.

02 Single sign-on, provisioning and access IT and identity

SAML 2.0, set up by people, not a toggle.

Single sign-on runs on your identity provider: Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, OneLogin, Ping Identity and most other SAML 2.0 IdPs. The integration team configures it with your metadata. There is no self-serve switch.

1 Your IdP

Create the SAML application and agree the identifier: work email or employee ID.

2 Metadata

Your IT sends IdP metadata. We send SP metadata, redirect URIs and the assertion endpoint.

3 Pilot

A small test group signs in and the round trip is verified.

4 Go live

SSO opens to everyone. Password sign-in stays on or comes off, your call.

On branding. SSO sends people to your IdP's own login page and branding. The Vantage Fit sign-in screen stays Vantage Fit. Full white-labelling is a separate build.

Other ways in

Your account manager sets the order.

  • PasswordReset links are single-use and expire in one hour.
  • Email OTPFour digits on mobile, six on web, valid for 10 minutes. SMS delivery for selected companies.
  • Google Sign-InGSuite enterprise domains.
  • Employee-ID loginWhitelist, by request.
  • Self-registrationOn when the work-email domain matches. A few enterprise domains are blocked.

Identity questions

Can we enable SSO ourselves?

No. SAML setup runs through the integration team, with a metadata exchange and a pilot group. Budget time for your IdP team too.

Can we change region later?

Not without a migration. Pick India, US, EU or UAE before onboarding closes. Security and compliance has the detail.
