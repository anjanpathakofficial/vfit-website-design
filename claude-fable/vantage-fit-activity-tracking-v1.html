What the employee opens

Two rings. One is always steps.

Ring 1 is steps, for everyone. Ring 2 shows active calories or active minutes, and which one appears is a company setting.

Defaults are 10,000 steps and 250 active calories, and a live challenge task overrides them. Tapping the rings opens the diary and the seven-day trends. Mindful-minute and sleep trends are Full Mode.
Switching Ring 2 to active minutes is a VC Support change, not a dashboard toggle. Decide it before launch week.
Data integrity

Honest numbers, not flattering ones.

A challenge result only holds up if the step data underneath it does.

Duplicate pushes are dropped

A sync at 95 to 105 percent of the previous day inside 18 hours is ignored. The rule was written for iOS 1.5.4 to 1.5.7.

Sync has a floor of 15 seconds

Reopening the app cannot push the same steps twice.

One primary device at a time

Fitbit or Garmin replaces Apple Health or Google Fit as the step source. A wrist and a pocket never both count.

Step capping is switched off

A normalization limit exists in the code, disabled for every company today. We would rather print that than imply a ceiling.

Rollout questions

Three things buyers ask in week one.

Do employees have to buy a tracker?

No. Steps come off the phone through Apple Health on iOS, and Google Fit or the selected source on Android. A wearable adds accuracy and data types a phone cannot see.

Someone joins in week two. Do they start at zero?

No. Historical step data uploads day by day with the hourly breakdown intact, so a late joiner is scored on the days walked.

Does any of this work in a browser?

The rings, trends and logging surface are in the mobile app. The web app covers challenges and leaderboards.
