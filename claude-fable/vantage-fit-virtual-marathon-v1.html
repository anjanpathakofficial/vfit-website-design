Every local clock

One event. Every time zone gets a full local day.

The last global event probably ran on headquarters' clock. Here are the four behaviors that decide it, and the two places to be careful.

Localized start

The event opens at local midnight on the start date, wherever the person is.

Localized end

It closes at local midnight on the end date, so a 30-day event is a true 30 calendar days for every participant.

Rewards in local currency

Values are configured in USD and shown in each employee's local currency, from a country-relevant gift card catalog.

A 3-day sync buffer

Late Fitbit and Garmin syncs still land before rankings settle.

Push notifications arrive at each recipient's corresponding local time, so stagger your sends. Report date filters run on the admin's time zone, not the participant's.

Questions before you put a date on the calendar.

Do employees need to run, or use GPS?

No. Distance is calculated from step count, not GPS. Employees do not need to use GPS workouts or run specific routes. Their regular daily steps are counted automatically, which is why a walker finishes the same event as a runner.

Do employees need a wearable?

No. The phone tracks steps through Apple Health on iOS and Google Fit on Android. Fitbit or Garmin take over when connected, and Vantage Fit uses one primary device at a time so nothing is counted twice.

Do employees have to sign up?

No. An audience rule enrolls everyone who matches, or you add people by search or CSV upload. There is no browse-and-join flow, so your participation number does not depend on a registration drive.

How does it work across time zones?

The event opens at local midnight on the start date and closes at local midnight on the end date, so every participant gets the same number of full days. Rewards are shown in each employee's local currency.

When are results final?

Results stay provisional for 3 days after the end date while late Fitbit and Garmin syncs land. Rankings, ranking bonuses, and certificates if your admin enabled them, settle after that buffer.
